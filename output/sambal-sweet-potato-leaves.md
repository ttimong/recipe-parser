# Sweet Potato Leaf Stir-Fry

## Ingredients
- 1 packet of sweet potato leaves (about 300g)
- 1½ tablespoons of sambal chilies
- 1 tablespoon ginger (from the bottle)
- Sprinkle of pepper
- $\frac{1}{2}$ teaspoon of cornflour mixed with a bit of water
- 3 tablespoons of oil
- 3 cloves of garlic, minced

## Instructions
- Heat oil in a pan.
- Add minced garlic and sauté for about 5 seconds.
- Add sweet potato leaves and stir until well mixed.
- Sprinkle pepper over the mixture.
- Add sambal chilies and ginger, and stir well.
- Pour in the cornflour mixture and let it boil.
- Turn off the heat once cooked.
