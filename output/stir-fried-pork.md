# Stir Fry Pork Tenderloin with Black Pepper

## Ingredients
- 300g tenderloin pork (sliced)
- 1 stalk spring onion (cut into inch pieces)

### Marinade
- 1 teaspoon sesame oil
- 1 tablespoon oyster sauce
- 1 tablespoon light soy sauce
- Pinch of black pepper powder
- ½ teaspoon corn flour

## Instructions
1. Marinate the pork for two hours with:
   - Sesame oil
   - Oyster sauce
   - Light soy sauce
   - Black pepper powder
   - Corn flour
2. Heat 3 tablespoons of cooking oil in a pan.
3. Add minced garlic and sauté for a while.
4. Add the marinated pork and stir-fry until cooked.
   - If the pork is too dry, add 3 tablespoons of water.
5. Lastly, add a pinch of black pepper powder and remove from heat.
