# Fried Xiao Bai Cai

## Ingredients
- 1 pack of Xiao Bai Cai
- 5 pieces of prawn
- 1 tablespoon of oyster sauce
- 1 teaspoon of light sauce
- 3 tablespoons of oil
- 3 cloves of garlic (minced)

## Instructions
1. Heat the cooking oil and garlic in a pan, then add the prawns.
2. Add the Xiao Bai Cai and stir-fry until cooked.
3. Add the oyster sauce and light sauce, stir well.
